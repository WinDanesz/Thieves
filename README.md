# Thieves

[![Build Status](https://github.com/Windanesz/Thieves/workflows/Build/badge.svg)](https://github.com/Windanesz/Thieves/actions)

A Minecraft mod that adds thieves and related items to the game.

## About

This mod introduces a new challenge to the game by adding thieves that will attempt to rob your base. They will spawn near your base and try to steal items from your chests. If they are successful, they will escape with your loot and store it in a hidden stash. You can then try to find the stash and recover your items.

## Features

- **Thieves:** A new mob that will try to rob your base.
- **Robberies:** Thieves will spawn in groups and attempt to steal items from your chests.
- **Loot Bags:** Thieves will store their stolen loot in loot bags.
- **Stashes:** Thieves will hide their loot in stashes that you can find and recover.
- **Master Thieves:** A more powerful version of the thief that can steal more items and is harder to kill.

## Installation

1.  Download the latest version of the mod from the [releases page](https://github.com/Windanesz/Thieves/releases).
2.  Install [Minecraft Forge](https://files.minecraftforge.net/net/minecraftforge/forge/).
3.  Place the downloaded `.jar` file in your `mods` folder.
